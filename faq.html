Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our products, customization and ordering process.

Steel Furniture

Q1. What types of steel furniture do you offer?

We offer a wide range of industrial steel furniture including workbenches, trolleys, cabinets, and more. Each piece is designed for durability and efficiency, catering to both industrial and commercial needs.

Q2. Can you customize steel furniture for my business?

Yes, we specialize in providing customized steel solutions based on your specific requirements. Whether it’s for a laboratory, hospital, or industrial setting, we can tailor furniture designs to suit your needs.

Q3. What is the lead time for steel furniture orders?

The lead time for custom steel furniture can vary depending on the complexity of the design and the quantity ordered. Generally, it takes 2–4 weeks for us to fulfill an order, but we can provide a more accurate timeline based on your specific request.

Q4. What is the warranty on your steel furniture?

Our steel furniture comes with a warranty of 1–3 years depending on the product. This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, ensuring your investment is protected.

Lockers and Cabinets

Q1. Do you offer customized lockers and cabinets?

Yes, we can customize lockers and cabinets for various applications, including offices, schools, hospitals, and industrial settings. Our designs prioritize security and functionality, with various locking mechanisms and sizes available.

Q2. How can I order lockers or cabinets?

You can place an order for lockers or cabinets by contacting us through our website or by calling our customer support. We’ll help you select the best options based on your needs and deliver them at your convenience.

Q3. Are your lockers and cabinets secure?

Yes, our lockers and cabinets are designed with security in mind. We offer various locking systems including mechanical locks, electronic locks, and keyless options to ensure the safety of your items.

Q4. Can I get large lockers for industrial use?

Yes, we provide lockers and cabinets in large sizes suitable for industrial use, designed to withstand heavy-duty requirements and provide ample storage for tools and equipment.

Laboratory and Hospital Essentials

Q1. Do you supply laboratory furniture?

Yes, we offer a range of laboratory essentials, including workbenches, lab cabinets, and storage units. All our products are made with durable materials to withstand the demands of a laboratory environment.

Q2. What hospital furniture do you provide?

We provide hospital essentials such as steel beds, trolleys, and storage cabinets. All our products are designed for comfort, durability, and functionality, ensuring the highest standards for healthcare environments.
