Steel furniture

What types of steel furniture do you offer?

We offer industrial steel furniture including workbenches, trolleys, cabinets and more, planned for durability and efficient use across industrial and commercial settings.

Can steel furniture be customized for a specific site?

Yes. Customized steel solutions can be developed around the operating area, workflow and dimensions of laboratories, hospitals and industrial facilities.

How is the lead time determined?

Lead time depends on design complexity and order quantity. A project-specific timeline can be confirmed after requirements are discussed.

What warranty coverage applies?

Warranty terms vary by product. The applicable coverage for each item should be confirmed with the sales team before ordering.

Lockers and cabinets

Can lockers and cabinets be configured for my facility?

Yes. Sizes, layouts and locking arrangements can be discussed for offices, schools, hospitals and industrial spaces.

How should I place an order?

Contact the team through this page or call customer support. We can help identify a suitable configuration and supply route.

What locking options are available?

Available locking mechanisms depend on the selected locker or cabinet model and can include mechanical, electronic or keyless options.

Are large industrial lockers available?

Large locker and cabinet configurations can be supplied for industrial use, subject to the selected model and project requirements.

Laboratory and healthcare

Do you supply laboratory furniture?

Yes. Laboratory essentials include workbenches, laboratory cabinets and storage units configured for specialist work environments.

What hospital furniture is available?

The range includes hospital essentials such as steel beds, trolleys and storage cabinets. Specifications should be confirmed for the application.
